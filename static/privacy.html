Privacy Details and AI usage

Spotify sign-in lets Adaptune search music, show your playlists and control playback. Tokens, playlist selection and recent track identifiers stay in temporary server memory. Disconnect Spotify to stop playback and delete this retained data. To revoke access at Spotify too, visit your Spotify app permissions. Closing a tab is not the same as disconnecting. Inactive server sessions expire within one hour.

Your Anthropic API key is sent to this server over HTTPS in deployed installations (HTTP is permitted only on the local development loopback address). It is kept in memory for no more than 30 minutes, and cleared on removal, session end, sign-out or session expiry. It is not written to the application database or application logs.

Song suggestions send only your independently typed preferences, current stress estimate and wellness goal to Anthropic. Spotify responses and listening history are not sent to Claude. Using AI incurs charges on the account associated with the supplied key.

Session analysis is optional. After your permission, relative timestamps, stress scores and the calculated average are sent to Anthropic. Email, account IDs and absolute session dates are not included. Anthropic processes these requests under its commercial terms and retention rules.

AI-generated interpretations may be inaccurate. They provide general wellness information, not diagnosis or medical advice. Sensor estimates and generated suggestions should not be treated as clinical measurements or guaranteed outcomes.

Saved session charts, stress datapoints and account email are stored in the configured Supabase project. Removing an API key or disconnecting Spotify does not delete saved Adaptune sessions. Karaoke uses an owner-supplied recording and transcript bundled locally. It sends no lyrics or song information to an AI or lyrics service and displays no synchronized lyrics.
